Privacy

Privacy notice

LaunchProof accepts token addresses and optional promotional text to generate an automated screening report. Do not submit private keys, passwords, personal data, or confidential material.

Data handling

Requests may be processed by the deployed serverless function and, when configured, an AI provider. API keys remain server-side. The MVP does not require an account.

Provider processing

When an AI provider is enabled, the server may send the submitted promotional text-derived claims and deterministic report signals to that provider to generate a summary. Do not submit personal, confidential, or sensitive information. Provider retention and processing are governed by that provider’s terms and configuration.

Logs and limits

Basic operational data such as request timing, errors, and rate-limit events may be retained for security and reliability. The service is designed not to require full user submissions in operational logs, but users should still avoid submitting sensitive information.

Third parties

External AI providers and hosting services may process data needed to operate the configured service. Review their terms before enabling production use.
